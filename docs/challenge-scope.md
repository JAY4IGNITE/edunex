# Challenge Scope

## Mandatory

- Integrate academic, attendance, LMS, engagement, placement, skills and feedback data.
- Calculate a Student Success Score.
- Identify academic risk.
- Identify placement risk.
- Provide an interactive analytics dashboard.
- Provide relevant student insights.

## Bonus

- Student segmentation.
- Explainable Success Score.

## Scope discipline

Coding and mock-interview performance are placement-readiness indicators. They are not separate coding/interview products.

Any supporting infrastructure such as an API, database, provenance layer, data-quality layer or upload workflow exists to support the required analytics and is not itself a new product feature.
