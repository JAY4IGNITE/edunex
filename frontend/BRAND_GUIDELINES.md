# EduNex identity and icon rules

## Identity

Use the custom three-tier E mark in `BrandMark.tsx` and `public/favicon.svg`. The forward-cut terminals are the identifying detail. Keep it monochrome, preserve its proportions, and leave at least one stroke-width of clear space. Do not place it inside an additional decorative tile or substitute a stock education icon. Pair it with the EduNex wordmark and the optional descriptor “Campus analytics”.

## Interface icons

Use the existing Lucide family at a consistent 1.8 stroke weight. Navigation uses a 20px optical size; metadata and actions use 14–18px. Keep clickable targets at least 44px on mobile even when the icon is smaller.

- Overview: dashboard layout.
- Students: contact directory.
- Risks: warning triangle.
- Segments: scatterplot, consistently in navigation and detail links.
- Insights and prediction: analytical chart.
- Data: database.
- Academic: marked book; attendance: checked calendar; LMS: learning screen; engagement: participants; placement: briefcase; skills: checklist; feedback: message.
- Internal navigation: right arrow. Reserve an external-link symbol for an actual external destination.
- Status: check, warning triangle, alert circle, or question mark accompanied by a visible label.

Icons should help identify an action, domain, or status. Omit decorative icons from headings, numeric values, and segment cards when they repeat information already visible. Use neutral icon colors by default; reserve accent for selection and semantic color for actual status. Avoid sparkles, neural/brain decorations, biometric fingerprints, arbitrary per-card symbols, and repeated colored icon tiles.

Decorative marks are hidden from assistive technology. Icon-only controls require an accessible name and visible focus. Labels remain visible in mobile navigation and when the full sidebar is expanded.
